Account

Signed in. Customer numbers are always masked here.

Alert settings

Alerts show on Home and as a bar on every page. These limits are saved in this browser.
Notifications and beeps only work while this portal is open in a browser tab or home-screen app. If the phone puts the tab to sleep, they pause.

Backup

Downloads every payment the portal holds, with customer numbers still masked. Keep a copy in Google Drive or email it to yourself.

How changes work

Retries, airtime checks and plan edits are sent to the phone, which double-checks them. Plan edits and offer forwarding wait for your approval on the phone. Phones sync about every 5 minutes.
